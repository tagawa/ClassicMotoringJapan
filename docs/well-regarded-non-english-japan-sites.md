## Well-regarded English sites that cover one theme of events in Japan

Scope: art, gigs, festivals, cherry blossoms, sumo, hiking, snow, etc. Check what people praise and criticise about each.

We found six comparable sites, five good models and one cautionary group. The evidence on reputation is thin: it mostly comes from the sites describing themselves, plus what I could see of how they work. I found almost no independent reader reviews; Tokyo Art Beat's app, for example, has only 6 US ratings.

| Site | Theme and shape | What earns trust | Weak spots |
|---|---|---|---|
| [japan-guide.com](https://www.japan-guide.com/blog/sakura15/150406_tokyo.html) cherry blossom reports | Dated reports for each spot, updated every 3 to 7 days in season | Staff visit in person ([profile](https://www.japan.go.jp/tomodachi/2017/spring-summer2017/guiding_japan_visitors.html)); the same status words every time, e.g. "opening (10-50%)", "beyond peak"; a published schedule of upcoming reports | None found |
| [Tokyo Art Beat](https://www.tokyoartbeat.com/en/articles/-/tokyo-art-beat-then-and-now-a-conversation) | Bilingual art exhibition listings since 2004, over 100,000 listings | Aimed to list every exhibition, praised as accurate and easy to use | Ran on volunteer effort for 16 years; the [founders moved on](https://tokyoartbeat.com/en/articles/-/tab-farewell-whats-next), the infrastructure aged, and in 2021 it was handed to a company. Many features are now [paid](https://www.tokyoartbeat.com/en/aboutSubscription), at ¥300 a month |
| [Inside Kyoto](https://www.insidekyoto.com/kyoto-festivals-events) | Kyoto festival calendar by one expert, a former Lonely Planet author | Decades of expertise and a named author | Strong opinions ("do NOT miss it"); entries vary, some missing times or with conflicting dates; earns from tours and affiliate links |
| [Hiking in Japan](https://hikinginjapan.com/about/) | Trail guides by one person since 2008 | Ad-free, paid for by the author with optional tips; readers' comments keep conditions current | Says openly that not every route was walked personally; the About page was last updated in 2021 |
| [Tokyo Cheapo](https://tokyocheapo.com/event-listing_ja/) events | Weekly roundups of what's on | Large reach, with a 33,000-subscriber newsletter | Sells featured placement for US$80 to US$200, and the page doesn't say whether paid listings are labelled |
| Sumo ticket guides, e.g. [this one](https://japantravelplanning.com/tokyo-sumo-wrestling-tickets/) | How to buy sumo tickets | Tells readers that official tickets sell out in minutes | The official site warns against resellers, yet the guide ranks affiliate-linked tours first and lists those resellers anyway. It also contradicts itself on whether same-day tickets are sold |

**What's common to the good ones:** they report facts seen first-hand, with dates, in the same fixed words. 

**What's common to the bad ones:** commissions steer the advice, the writing drifts into opinion, the same page contradicts itself, or a solo or volunteer project burns out.
