About

Classic Motoring Japan lists classic car events in Japan in English: when they run, where to watch, what admission costs, how to get there, and which cars will be there.

Where the facts come from

  • Organisers announce their events in Japanese.
  • Every date and detail here is translated from the organiser's own site, flyer or announcement by someone who reads Japanese, not scraped or machine-translated.
  • Each page shows the date it was last checked against that source, and links to it.
  • Dates an organiser has not confirmed are marked Not confirmed. Cancelled editions stay listed, marked Cancelled.
  • Lists of cars are the organiser's, as of the date shown on the list.

What is not here

  • Every classic car event in Japan: only those with an official source to check against.
  • Personal data: lists of cars name the car, never the driver or owner.
  • Tickets or bookings.

Not affiliated with any event or organiser.

Reporting an error

Wrong dates and details can be reported as a GitHub issue, which needs a free GitHub account.
